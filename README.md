## What it does

`main.py` writes a two-line explanation of a traffic jam to `/output/entry.txt`.
Line 1 explains the traffic jam to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built
on a single analogy. Line 2 says in one sentence what that analogy maps and
what it leaves out.

## The entry

> A traffic jam is like a whole school trying to squeeze through one tiny classroom door at the same time, everybody pushing forward and nobody getting anywhere fast.
>
> It maps the bottleneck and the waiting, but it leaves out the engines, the traffic lights, and the fact that cars cannot just turn sideways and slip through like kids can.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is UTF-8 text of 337 characters across exactly two lines, inside
the 1 to 400 character gate.
